erica

A skill that coaches you to explain an idea so people listen. Give it an idea and it picks a structure, writes a script, and draws the central picture. Give it a draft and it scores the draft out of 20.

4structures
9beats
20point rubric
2modes
Two modes

Coach a new idea, or review a draft

The skill asks for the idea, the listener, the outcome, and the format. It asks once, and only for what is missing.

COACH

From idea to script

Picks one of four structures, checks that the idea has a core, a surprise, a name, a turn to the listener, and an exercise, then writes the skeleton and a 900 to 1,200 word script. If a drawing tool is available it draws one central diagram.

REVIEW

From draft to fixes

Scores the draft against ten checks, names the weakest beat, gives at most three fixes ranked by leverage, and rewrites one example of the top fix.

Structures

Four ways to build an explanation

The skill picks one per piece and says why. It does not mix them.

StructureUse it forSpine
Story-vehicleA behavior or effect in peopleOdd hook, a story the audience already knows, the idea it shows, why it works, an exercise on paper, hand back.
Myth-bustA belief that holds people backStriking scene, name the myth, the research behind it, who benefits from it, the antidote, an invitation to act.
PlaybookA skill the listener must runCost of not doing it, name the trap, numbered steps with short names, rules of thumb.
Case breakdownA person or company that wonA puzzle, N strategies each with a name and proof, then a blueprint for the listener's own case.
Beats

Nine beats inside any structure

Each beat has one job. A draft that skips a beat scores lower on the rubric.

01

Scene first

A concrete scene or an odd question before any concept.

02

Puzzle

The scene breaks an expectation.

03

Name it

A short name and a one-sentence definition.

04

Everywhere

Three to six fast examples from unrelated fields.

05

Mechanism

One plain reason it works.

06

Open loop

Promise the how-to early, delay it, end each section on a question.

07

Steps and exercise

Three to six steps and one exercise on paper.

08

Chain formula

One line a stranger can repeat tomorrow.

09

Hand back

End on the listener. No recap.

Rubric

Twenty points, three bands

Each of ten checks scores 0, 1, or 2. The weakest beat gets fixed first.

Install

Copy one folder

Put the skill in your Claude Code skills directory, restart, then type /erica or ask "help me explain" followed by your idea.

cp -R skills/erica ~/.claude/skills/

Optional: with an Excalidraw MCP server connected, the skill draws one black-and-white diagram. Without it, the skill describes the diagram in text.
