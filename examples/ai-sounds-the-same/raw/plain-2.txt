Every business has two kinds of knowledge: what's written down, and what lives in someone's head.

The second kind walks out the door when people leave, goes on holiday, or just gets too busy to answer the same question for the fifth time.

A shared knowledge base fixes that. New hires ramp up faster. Decisions stop getting remade from scratch. Customers get consistent answers, no matter who picks up the phone.

It doesn't need to be fancy. Start with your top 10 recurring questions and document the answers.

Your team's expertise is an asset. Make sure it belongs to the company, not just to individuals.
