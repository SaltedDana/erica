Ask a new hire a simple question: "Where do I find our pricing process?" If the answer is "Ask Sarah," you have a problem.

A shared knowledge base fixes that. Processes, decisions, and lessons learned sit in one searchable place. New hires ramp faster. Teams stop reinventing the wheel. You stop being the bottleneck.
