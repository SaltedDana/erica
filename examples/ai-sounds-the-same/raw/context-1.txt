Our best accountant left in June. She took twelve years of knowing which clients pay late, which ones need a phone call instead of an email, and why one VAT return gets filed differently.

None of it was written down.

It took us four months to piece back together. Some of it we never did.

So now there's a shared knowledge base. It's not pretty. It's a folder and a lot of plain pages.

But the next time someone leaves, they'll only take their mug.
