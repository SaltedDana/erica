Every business has two kinds of knowledge: what's written down, and what lives in someone's head.

The second kind is expensive. It walks out the door when people leave, slows down every new hire, and forces your best people to answer the same questions again and again.

A shared knowledge base fixes that. Processes, decisions, client details, and how-tos live in one place everyone can find.

The payoff is real: faster onboarding, fewer mistakes, and teams that don't stall waiting for answers.

You don't need a perfect system. Start by writing down the five things people ask most.
