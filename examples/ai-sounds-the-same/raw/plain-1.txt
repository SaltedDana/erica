Every business runs on knowledge. The question is where it lives.

If the answer is "in Sarah's head" or "somewhere in a Slack thread from March," you have a risk. When Sarah goes on vacation, or leaves, the knowledge goes with her.

A shared knowledge base fixes that. New hires ramp up faster. Customers get consistent answers. Your team stops answering the same questions again and again, and spends that time on real work.

It also means your business no longer depends on any single person.

Start small: document one process this week. Your future team will thank you.
