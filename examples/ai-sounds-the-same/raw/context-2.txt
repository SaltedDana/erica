Our last hire asked where the VAT workings live. I said "Sarah's head." Sarah was on holiday in Crete.

That's not a knowledge base. That's a hostage situation.

Twelve people, one firm, and half of what we know sits in someone's inbox or someone's memory. Fine until they're ill, leave, or just don't pick up.

So we started writing things down. One page per process. Plain English. Searchable.

It took three weeks. It saves us about an hour a day.

If your best answer is "ask Sarah," you don't have a system. You have Sarah.
