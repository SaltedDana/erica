Every business has a "that one person who knows" problem.

Ask Sarah how a client was onboarded. Ask Tom where the pricing exceptions live. Ask whoever's been here longest why we do it that way.

When knowledge lives in heads and inboxes, every absence becomes a bottleneck and every departure becomes a loss.

A shared knowledge base fixes this. New hires ramp faster. Answers stop depending on who's online. Decisions get made on the same facts.

It's not about paperwork. It's about building a company that doesn't break when one person is out.
