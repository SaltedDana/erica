Our shared drive has 41 folders. Three are named "Misc."

Last month a new starter asked how we handle VAT on a client who'd gone dormant. Four people gave four answers. All confident. Two were wrong.

That's not a people problem. That's a "the answer lives in someone's head" problem. And heads go on holiday.

So we're writing it down. One page per question. Plain English. Searchable.

It's not glamorous. But nobody has asked me the dormant-VAT question since.

If it only lives in your head, it's not a process. It's a rumour.
